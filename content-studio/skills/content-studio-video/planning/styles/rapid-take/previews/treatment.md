# Rapid Take treatment example

Planning evidence, not a rendered video. The style registry lists `rapid-take` with formats
**video only** for fast, evidence-led tech news with meme punctuation. Read the Rapid Take reference before authoring.

Review path:

1. Confirm the style is enabled in the selected project and supports the requested format.
2. Attach source evidence to every factual beat.
3. Render a short representative clip and inspect first, middle, and last frames.
4. Record human approval before the editable timeline.

No renderer is claimed by this planning skill. Unsupported formats must stop before export.
