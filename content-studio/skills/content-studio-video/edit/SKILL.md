# content-studio-video-edit

The default story edit is performed by the user. [AutoCut](autocut/SKILL.md) runs only on explicit request. Use [clip edit](clip-edit/SKILL.md), [transitions](transitions/SKILL.md), [reframe](reframe/SKILL.md), [color](color/SKILL.md), [restoration](restoration/SKILL.md), [upscale](upscale/SKILL.md), [reflection](reflection/SKILL.md), and [motion graphics](motion-graphics/SKILL.md) for diagnosis and bounded review candidates after the mandatory precheck. Permanent decisions enter the [delivery timeline](../timeline/SKILL.md) before separate human approval and one final render. Check its method registry before promising an effect; an unsupported adapter blocks final export. Each leaf skill is directly callable after its own approved precheck.
