# Headless Blender craft and style

This Content Studio route adapts useful ideas from [Blender Skills at pinned commit 904f095](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/tree/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d), licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/LICENSE). The upstream skills are guidance, not executable scene builders. Content Studio's production precheck, evidence, rights, and human review gates control actual work.

Choose a subject discipline first: prop/hard surface, character/creature, vegetation, vehicle, or environment. Block the silhouette and scale before details. Keep moving parts independent, name their pivots and parent relations, and match real references when physical accuracy matters. Record when internals or motion are illustrative.

Choose one dominant visual style, then optional mood, world, genre, and era cues. A style may require different geometry, materials, light response, animation timing, camera, or compositing. The [low-poly lamp treatment](../blender/lowpoly-review.md) shows a distinct geometry and camera recipe against the smooth lamp benchmark. Show the resulting proposal to the user in the editable production plan. Use `python blender/scripts/catalog.py plan "<request>" [--style <id>] [--world <id>] [--genre <id>]` to shortlist guidance. The scene-aware planner also accepts `blender_genre` alongside `blender_subject`, `blender_style`, and `blender_world`; it can infer the reviewed action-combat arena from a combat-arena prompt with no named style. The [capability manifest](../blender/capabilities.json) only returns a builder for a demonstrated subject, intent, and compatible style; other requests remain `Catalogued`.

For video output, author trusted Blender Python exposing `make_scene(spec, spec_dir)` and use the [version 2 shot contract](../blender/shot-contract.md) for new routes. Use the [existing frame preview](../engineering-3d/references/headless-preview.md) for initial, action, and held result frames. Render the same builder through `blender/scripts/render_shot.py`, which saves an editable `.blend`, encoded MP4, and hash report. Compare the exact motion and the narrated composition with [object scoring](../../review/critique/references/object-scoring.md) where appropriate. Score every full gallery candidate; only the best technically valid GIF appears in the README.

Import meshes, textures, or rigs only with approved rights and recorded provenance. The current [gallery examples](../blender/README.md) use original procedural assets. The [desk lamp craft benchmark](../blender/lamp-review.md) also checks meter scale, non-destructive mesh structure, and an editable pivot. For an asset handoff, the [Blender QA pass](../blender/qa-review.md) checks a saved scene, exact MP4 and GIF, triangle budget, and inspected angles before a ship verdict. A rendered example establishes its described action, not every craft or style named in the catalog. Unity, Unreal, and Godot exports remain roadmap entries until their adapters and round-trip checks exist.
