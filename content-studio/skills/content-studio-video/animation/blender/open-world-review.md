# Open-world biome crossing review

The [original Blender builder](examples/open_world_crossing.py) lays out meadow and badlands around a river bridge, a tall destination tower, a cache, and spaced discovery crumbs. The [pinned upstream open-world direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/genre-open-world-sandbox/SKILL.md) calls for landmarks, biome borders, traversal toys, and discovery cues without clutter. This authored diorama shows one traversal lane; it does not provide a streaming world, terrain system, or playable sandbox.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates passed the 72-frame, 960×540, 24 fps shot contract. The traveler and tower were previewed separately before the full renders. Encoded MP4 frames at the starting meadow, approach, bridge crossing, and badlands arrival were inspected, along with both GIFs' final frames. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. Both saved scenes passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_open_world.py): a distinct two-biome border, bridge across the full river, tall tower and beacon, three sparse crumbs and cache, a parented traveler, and exact output settings. In v2, the traveler reaches the river at frame 40 and the badlands at frame 72; the beacon grows at the destination. Character and environmental assets are simple illustrative geometry.
