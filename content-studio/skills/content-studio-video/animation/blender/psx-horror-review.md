# PSX horror CRT hall review

The [PSX horror builder](examples/psx_horror.py) creates an original narrow hall with one analog CRT as its narrative payoff. It differs from the [psychological horror corridor](horror-review.md) through a 320×240, 4:3 source raster, flat 228-triangle scene geometry, a self-contained 64×64 wall atlas sampled with `Closest`, a limited desaturated green palette, low draw-distance fog, and a lightly wavering camera. The camera advances toward the CRT as its phosphor brightens. This demonstrates `psx-horror-style` for this authored hall, not a general PS1 renderer or game-engine pipeline.

The exact Blender 5.3 MP4 is 72 frames at 320×240 and 24 fps, with SHA-256 `c5351a5adee92fe6cf4cd4f9cb1e383b1b25421092f8f71d123b70547571812a`. The selected [optimized GIF](previews/psx-horror.gif) is 480×360, 24 frames, SHA-256 `5c2bbdd5064c7062bffe64e697cfda4839ba421b65e28d4c4a257e8d06cf4d1f`. It uses nearest-neighbor enlargement and a 64-color palette to preserve the source pixel edges. The sole full-render candidate scores **78/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json); selection is provisional until a competing full render is scored. Early bounded previews placed the CRT too far away; it and the camera move were revised before the full candidate render.

The exact encoded MP4 first, action, and held frames were visually inspected. The CRT grows clearly during a 2.7 m push and its green emission increases from 0.1 to 3.4. The wall atlas reads as coarse blocks, and the selected GIF retains that pixel structure. This treatment has no affine texture warp, vertex wobble, VHS overlay, audio, or interactive controls; the finish score is therefore 3/5.

The saved `.blend` passed the [PSX structural audit](scripts/audit_psx.py): 320×240 Standard-color render, packed 64×64 atlas with nearest filtering, 228 flat scene triangles without bevel or subdivision, positive fog density, one CRT narrative prop, a 2.7 m push, visible camera sway, and increasing CRT emission. The [pinned upstream PSX horror guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/psx-horror-style/SKILL.md) informed the low-poly, texture, palette, fog, and analog prop choices; the code and assets are original. The audit report, MP4, editable `.blend`, and frame cache remain in ignored `tmp/`.
