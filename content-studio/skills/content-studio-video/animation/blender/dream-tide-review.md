# Dream tide surreal mood review

The [original Blender builder](examples/dream_tide.py) chooses one dream/surreal lane: a rose doorway standing over a lavender mirror sea without a wall, eight unsupported stairs, an oversized brass key, a tiny paper boat, and a concealed lunar mark. The [pinned upstream dream-surreal direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/dream-surreal-mood/SKILL.md) calls for bent logic, scale play, soft physics, and symbolic props. This is distinct from the existing unsettling [dream playground style](dream-review.md); it demonstrates one pastel threshold scene, not a general surreal renderer.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 63/100** (subject 4, motion 1, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `dd2e3416c8bebe071df87c8d339d79e6d3df014d9b63a3117442a29fa899ca71`; its GIF SHA-256 is `de0549dcc353e64c95997b0ce40d1bb73a63f3f3b6895cd632a66727367efd26`. The objects establish scale and impossible architecture, but barely move.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/dream-tide.gif) scores higher because the stairs lift, the tiny boat drifts, the rose door swings aside to reveal a luminous lunar mark, and the camera approaches. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `73542144b56da38f048be17d392d267e0a9611426a6115b2ac44241b92f4fa61`; its GIF SHA-256 is `05cd7e65159dd28d1dd175604eab3d4f7bf343374ebc8df7316412d7ed3131b7`. The doorway was previewed alone at closed, partly open, and revealed states, then verified in the full scene. Exact encoded opening, action, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_dream_tide.py): eight unsupported steps, giant key relative to the boat, keyed door and boat, concealed emitted lunar mark, pastel area lights, and camera motion. Both full renders passed shot-contract and exact encoded-frame checks. The sea is a glossy illustrative plane; it does not simulate tides or fluid physics.
