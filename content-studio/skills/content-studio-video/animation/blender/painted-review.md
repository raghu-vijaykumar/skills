# Hand-painted teacup review

The [original teacup builder](examples/painted_teacup.py) wraps a procedural, brush-like 256×256 wildflower albedo around a simple 48-segment UV cup. The ceramic, rim, and saucer stay matte; the cup rotates to reveal the artwork as three steam curls grow from the rim. This places brushwork on the hero's surface, distinct from the exaggerated house and roof silhouette of the [stylized windmill](stylized-review.md). It demonstrates one authored `hand-painted-style` prop shot, not a general texture painter or style transfer system.

The selected Blender 5.3 MP4 is 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps, SHA-256 `69a0e6f4b385a9b0383a461e154e21a2ef54e38f2ef4d70826b7d71782da540d`. Its [optimized GIF](previews/painted-teacup.gif) is 480×270, 24 frames at 8 fps, SHA-256 `43719137640190c2cd2681fd7fc4e856c14aea6e6425f6080ee11dfead2c209a`. It scores **82/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json) and is the best passing candidate.

Both full outputs were scored. The first MP4, SHA-256 `ef39309bb5643e79683cda9430d86bb0861d8c52a2c0b29bcf58032cdd8b8aaa`, and its GIF, SHA-256 `3e98de9ac85fae446ce7f9d64c38e54ff3ddfbd3ab76dd6b7c869b107ef9c447`, scored **78/100**. It passed encoding and the saved-scene audit, but the pale untreated background reduced composition. The selected revision added broad bristle variation to the flower pigment and a packed 128×128 background wash; its composition improved from 3/5 to 4/5. The rejected GIF and both full MP4s remain in ignored `tmp/`.

Exact encoded first, action, and held frames and both GIF strips were inspected. The wildflower motif stays readable while the handle moves from the front toward the right. The steam grows from an invisible first pose into three distinct curls without leaving the frame. The 2D brush marks are code-authored and the flower shapes are intentionally simple, limiting subject, motion, composition, and finish to 4/5. The final GIF is a compressed preview; the full MP4 carries the original surface detail.

Both saved `.blend` files passed the [painted structural audit](scripts/audit_painted.py): editable cup and handle pivot, 48 UV ring segments, packed 256×256 albedo, 0.94 ceramic roughness and zero metallic response, fixed orthographic camera, 1.26-radian cup turn, and three steam curls growing from 0.001× to 1.0× with roots at the rim. The audit sampled 83 distinct rounded albedo colors in the first scene and 97 in the selected scene; the latter also retained its packed background wash. Evidence integrity is 5/5. The [pinned upstream hand-painted guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/hand-painted-style/SKILL.md) informed the albedo, matte material, and geometry choices; all artwork and animation are original.
