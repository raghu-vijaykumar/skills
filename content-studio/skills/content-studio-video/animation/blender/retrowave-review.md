# Neon retrowave highway review

The [original Blender builder](examples/retrowave_highway.py) chooses one neon-retrofuturism lane: an open cyan perspective grid beneath a striped peach sunset, violet mountain silhouettes, a spare magenta gateway, and a satin-chrome coupe driving toward the horizon. The [pinned upstream neon-retrofuturism direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/neon-retrofuturism/SKILL.md) calls for sunset gradients, grid perspective, chrome and neon, and an 80s future memory. This scene uses those cues without a cyberpunk city; it demonstrates one outrun lane rather than a general retrowave renderer.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 67/100** (subject 4, motion 1, composition 5, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 82/100** (4, 4, 5, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `01d0260cdb84af2bdfd15a7a5303dc1ff825f5ec7ac49e5ee7da2656c722e5b4`; its GIF SHA-256 is `e45ceafe988a6fb8e3ccffdca24caff80aa583748683326116ff29c083e289d4`. The horizon composition reads, but the coupe barely moves.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/retrowave-highway.gif) scores higher because the chrome coupe travels 7.2 m toward the sunset, its four wheels turn, and the camera follows. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `ac80b0704ac4b2a922061a63cc8dcc9ec0bbd86a1e99400322f702eb4b4c15f9`; its GIF SHA-256 is `512b44a73c5fa7be1d2a5de8829e35dc6bd4196f0673086794010a2ec07db437`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. The rejected output remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The coupe was previewed alone before the full road scene. Exact encoded opening, action, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_retrowave.py): 31 longitudinal and 23 cross-grid lines, five sun stripes, two mountain ridges, chrome and emitted cyan/magenta materials, four separate wheels, and keyed car travel. Both full renders passed shot-contract and exact encoded-frame checks. The coupe and mountain silhouettes are deliberately stylized; the shot does not claim period-accurate car design, physically measured road scale, or a simulated sun.
