# Comic rocket panel review

The [original rocket builder](examples/comic_rocket.py) makes a moving 3D hero inside an inked panel. Six printed speed lines, a packed 256×256 blue screentone, a timed angular burst, strong contours, and filled `WHOOSH!` lettering make a comic-book treatment distinct from the [cartoon mascot](cartoon-review.md). The rocket travels left to right and its flame changes length. This demonstrates `comic-book-style` for one authored rocket panel, not a general comic conversion or vehicle asset pipeline.

The exact Blender 5.3 MP4 is 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps, SHA-256 `b6fa33ddddb85883336922df31edbf212495b01bd75773f6f02e941648e48d68`. The selected [optimized GIF](previews/comic-rocket.gif) is 480×270, 24 frames, SHA-256 `e0d2913c87096117d2017f3fbff534861d9c725f525ba785a602618c5136e924`. The sole full-render candidate scores **82/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json); selection is provisional until a competing full render is scored. Bounded previews reduced oversized screen dots, filled the previously faint lettering, hid an initial burst dot, and gave the rocket clearance at the far edge before the full candidate render.

Exact encoded first, action, and held frames were inspected. A sampled GIF strip retains the rocket travel, emerging burst, and `WHOOSH!` cue at 8 fps. The printed dots remain visible without overpowering the hero. The model and print treatment are intentionally simple: the rocket has no physical propulsion simulation, the speed lines stay fixed, and the panel is a 3D scene with a packed texture rather than a compositor halftone pass. Those limits keep subject, motion, composition, and finish at 4/5.

The saved `.blend` passed the [comic structural audit](scripts/audit_comic.py): 2.4 px ink contour, packed 256×256 halftone, six speed lines, four border bars, 24-triangle impact burst, rocket motion from −1.28 m to 1.18 m, flame length increase at frame 36, and burst and lettering visibility after their cues. The [pinned upstream comic guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/comic-book-style/SKILL.md) and [ink-tone reference](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/comic-book-style/references/ink-tone.md) informed these choices; the rocket, print, and panel are original. The MP4, editable `.blend`, audit, and frame cache remain in ignored `tmp/`.
