# RPG quest hub review

The [original Blender builder](examples/rpg_quest_hub.py) chooses one RPG direction: a split forest and ochre-highland guild hub, an exclamation quest cue, warrior sword and shield, mage hat and staff, and separate common, rare, and epic loot colors. The [pinned upstream RPG direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/genre-rpg/SKILL.md) calls for region identity, quest hubs, loot-rarity language, and class-readable gear. This is a stylized tabletop-sized scene, not a complete RPG world or item system.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates passed the shot contract at **72 frames, 960×540, 24 fps**. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 63/100** (subject 4, motion 1, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `f8d707867d8b7ab16c1dfc99e51650a0df6b3fa7bc268b0f795258564a1beeca`; its GIF SHA-256 is `46d70cc63a3b0338189dcf73641b15fd26a4f339b18e029cbb3ef93d131d02b8`. The hub reads, but the quest cue and chest barely move.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/rpg-quest-hub.gif) shows the quest marker lifting, a blue rare chest opening, a violet gem rising, and a camera push across the two regions. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `eca4fdef21b9135c6d06d83532b3e29af50f0281830f7d3c598390fe33d2dc5f`; its GIF SHA-256 is `ce195d874d780b389733741947ec6ccd3020f2013cf577fa2b25f8e505e74e8f`. The common, rare, and epic loot props and opening chest were previewed alone before full rendering. Encoded hub, quest, and rarity MP4 frames and both GIFs' final frames were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_rpg.py): three forest pines, three highland standing stones, a parented quest marker, separate warrior and mage gear, three distinct rarity materials, a hinged chest lid, and exact keyed poses. The figures do not walk or interact with the chest; the gem motion is authored keyframes rather than a game inventory event.
