# Ruined causeway Soulslike review

The [original Blender builder](examples/soulslike_causeway.py) stages a small sword-bearer on an ash-stone causeway between broken tall columns. A warm bonfire marks the safe side; a monumental pale gate opens to reveal a much larger red adversary and long blade. The [pinned upstream Soulslike direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/genre-soulslike/SKILL.md) calls for melancholy ruins, bonfires, fog gates, shortcut landmarks, and readable threats. This shot demonstrates the ruin, bonfire, gate, and threat cues. It does not demonstrate a shortcut route or playable combat.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates passed the shot contract at **72 frames, 960×540, 24 fps**. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 58/100** (subject 3, motion 1, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `75bdf3ffab00e62586540089ff0086cb8f938fbdeed9466cf858ecf390371376`; its GIF SHA-256 is `dbcf779618ee44554be999d756878094a1e878c4e43ea2f70fbf115ab3ec0fd0`. Its traveler barely moves, the gate stays sealed, and the adversary never appears.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/soulslike-causeway.gif) shows the traveler pass the bonfire, gate panels part, and a red threat appear as the camera moves closer. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `2c2cf1750cae28373f9488e4fed1accd390ab7dc28ea85f1853623b30a3a72ec`; its GIF SHA-256 is `9845525b46309062cf536ed7bcd3ff3886a9879833b306c48f542220ff294046`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270; the rejected output remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The traveler, bonfire, gate, and adversary were previewed alone before full rendering. Encoded MP4 frames at the start, bonfire approach, gate opening, and final state, plus both GIFs' final frames, were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_soulslike.py): three tall ruined columns, hearth and wood, a paired gate under a monumental arch, contrasting actor silhouettes and a blade, and exact keyed poses. The fog is represented by solid pale moving panels rather than volumetric simulation; the actors and stone remain simplified.
