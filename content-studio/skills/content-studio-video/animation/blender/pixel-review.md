# Pixel treasure chest review

The [original chest builder](examples/pixel_chest.py) makes a 3D prop with packed, nearest-filtered 16×16 front and lid prints, a fixed orthographic camera, an eight-color authored material palette, and a hinged reveal. A gold square and four sparks rise in held positions. It uses printed texels and a reduced display raster, distinct from the grid-built geometry in the [voxel lamp](voxel-review.md). This demonstrates one authored `pixel-art-style` prop shot, not a general pixel-art renderer or automatic texture conversion.

The selected Blender 5.3 MP4 has 48 frames at 320×240 and 12 fps, SHA-256 `902323565c95baafc75dec60360e8e0861fcd480e6c2fd4abc373ae3e261f9a1`. Its [optimized GIF](previews/pixel-chest.gif) has 24 frames at 480×360 and 6 fps, SHA-256 `af4364960afceb5c84c39c996e4c71f95fdf461055e7ee7f41856533058598de`. The GIF was made from the exact MP4 by nearest downsampling to 160×120, nearest upscaling to 480×360, and a maximum 24-color palette without dithering. It scores **82/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json) and is the highest-scoring technically valid result.

Three outputs were scored. The first full MP4, SHA-256 `f29e46d8d563f82d7922beb3f7ae6d516596a8bef97369f3d38d9190ca1583a9`, passed encoding but its saved `.blend` discarded two unused palette material records, so its structural audit failed. Its direct-resize GIF, SHA-256 `3e0097195064e090625d7b92bc0cb8ccdb53ab9ba4d853f41848197c1db28f74`, scored **73/100**: edges and texture read less like coarse pixel art. Its nearest-downsampled GIF, SHA-256 `af4364960afceb5c84c39c996e4c71f95fdf461055e7ee7f41856533058598de`, scored **78/100** with better texture and silhouettes but the same audit failure. The corrected second render retained all eight palette material records and produced the same selected GIF pixels. The earlier candidates and MP4s remain in ignored `tmp/`.

Exact encoded first, action, and held frames and both GIF strips were inspected. The printed lid and side remain legible; the lid opens substantially between frames 1 and 48, while the square treasure and sparks emerge. The simple chest geometry and restrained background limit subject, composition, motion, and finish to 4/5. The corrected saved `.blend` passed the [pixel structural audit](scripts/audit_pixel.py): 320×240 at 12 fps, fixed orthographic camera, two packed nearest 16×16 prints, eight palette records, four sparks, lid angle from 0 to −1.12 radians, treasure height from 0.72 to 1.35 m, and first spark height from 0.84 to 1.43 m. Evidence integrity scores 5/5 for the selected candidate. The MP4 retains some raster edge shading; the GIF's nearest scaling is a deliberate compact display treatment, not a claim of an eight-color final video.
