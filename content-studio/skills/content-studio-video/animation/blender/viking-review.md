# Viking longship review

The [original Blender builder](examples/viking_longship.py) chooses one historical period lane: a stylized Viking longship on a rocky fjord, inspired by ninth to eleventh century Scandinavian craft. The [pinned upstream historical-worlds direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/historical-worlds/SKILL.md) asks for an identified era and period-aware silhouettes and materials. The modeled narrow raised-end hull, overlapping strake cues, round shields, oars, timber mast, and undyed and madder-red wool square sail establish this lane without an invented horned helmet. It does not claim archaeological accuracy or cover the other historical groups.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 64/100** (subject 4, motion 2, composition 4, finish 2, evidence 5) and **v2 73/100** (4, 3, 5, 2, 5). The ship was previewed alone, then in the fjord. A first camera preview cropped the bow on the final beat; the final camera follow was corrected before full rendering. Exact encoded opening, middle, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_viking.py): 12 round shields, eight oars, six strake cues, four red wool sail stripes, a narrow raised-end hull, a period label, and no helmet object. The selected ship moves from x=-1.75 m to x=1.75 m while the camera follows. Both full renders passed the shot contract and exact encoded-frame checks. The boxy rocks, flat water, static sail, and simplified hull limit the finish; this is a period-inspired animation example rather than a vessel simulation.
