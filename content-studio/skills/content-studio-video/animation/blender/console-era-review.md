# PS1 and modern-console flight review

The [original Blender builder](examples/console_era_flight.py) presents the same flight beat on two panels. The left ship uses a 52-vertex chunky silhouette, a nearest-filtered 16×16 texture, flat materials, and physical scanlines. The right ship uses 1,388 mesh vertices, smooth beveled geometry, and metallic PBR materials. The [pinned upstream era direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/visual-console-eras/SKILL.md) calls for era-specific polygon count, filtering, lighting, and post treatment. This example covers a PS1-like and PS5-like visual comparison. PS2–PS4, arcade, DOS, VHS, and film-grain treatments still need separate evidence; the models are illustrative and do not emulate historical hardware.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates passed the 72-frame, 960×540, 24 fps shot contract. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 62/100** (subject 3, motion 1, composition 4, finish 4, evidence 5) and **v2 77/100** (4, 3, 4, 4, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `06791d93b8f654729458982ab8a8b4c5b6b92cdc0a747f618e154d14f6cd5912` and its GIF SHA-256 is `d4d8f8aee183ee03a91b1c2da142cbb360a1b3e4e42dc726430f167b382e9972`. Its static display cannot show equivalent motion. The [selected v2 GIF](previews/console-era-flight.gif) has MP4 SHA-256 `ecf762a4999c85697ff11eb26d541db992d9282695f6ec2875e5d7583ff525a7` and GIF SHA-256 `552edc819f5175a42afbe2093805d0b45bfa418283584acfb5c64f8c3fa29cd1`. Rejected media remains in ignored `tmp/`.

Each ship was previewed alone before full rendering. Encoded MP4 frames at the start, bank onset, held pose, and end were inspected, along with both GIFs' last frames. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. Both saved scenes passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_console_era.py): topology gap, nearest texture dimensions and filter, PBR values, physical scanlines, labels, exact delivery settings, and matched ship motion in v2. The PS1 scanlines are scene geometry rather than a measured CRT post process, and the PS5-like finish is a Blender material demonstration.
