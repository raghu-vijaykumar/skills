# Forest cabin indie horror review

The [original Blender builder](examples/indie_cabin.py) chooses one coherent lane: a first-person approach to a timber cabin through chunky firs and red trail markers. The [pinned upstream indie horror direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/indie-horror-aesthetics/SKILL.md) lists several reference families and recommends choosing a lane. This original scene demonstrates the cabin/forest lane only; it does not claim hospital, office, PSX, or named-franchise reproduction.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 68/100** (subject 4, motion 2, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `84683fdcfbed184b306104f9c15a8e5f0a4fbfc5f9b432624ecf2c6d875f320c`; its GIF SHA-256 is `7139f52b5f6720c3739d644f2f81f8c39d8f4d4694428fce483cdd7fc837141f`. Its lit window and slight camera drift make the story static.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/indie-cabin.gif) scores higher because the camera follows the red markers toward the porch, the warm window fails, and cool ambient light keeps the cabin inspectable. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `ef2404f76f5669fdd02a4f885f77fc269101a82db7c73e5e386be74d29b75bc4`; its GIF SHA-256 is `332b798a4fd1e11e943a2c944eaa5de40bd7906b7e0f6baa04b34b2ab6dadaef`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. The rejected output remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The cabin was previewed alone and then in the full forest composition. Exact encoded opening, action, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_indie.py): 30 indexed fir trunks, eight red markers, pitched roof, first-person camera with parented spot flashlight, practical window light, and v2 blackout. Both full renders passed the shot contract and exact MP4 frame and size checks. The flashlight contributes illumination but its cone is not volumetrically visible. Trees and siding are deliberately simple modeled game geometry; this is an art direction example rather than a real location reconstruction.
