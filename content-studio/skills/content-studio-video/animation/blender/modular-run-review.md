# Modular ability-gate run review

The [original Blender builder](examples/modular_gate.py) stages three equal room modules: a green moss start, a neutral stone ability chamber, and a violet crystal destination. A cyan orb matches a locked blue gate; after the traveler collects it, the gate slides apart and the traveler enters the crystal room. Red pointed and gold round props mark two contrasting run modifiers. The [pinned upstream metroidvania and roguelike direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/genre-metroidvania-roguelike/SKILL.md) calls for modular rooms, ability gates, biome identity, and readable modifiers. This is one authored route, not procedural generation, a playable ability system, or actual run-to-run state.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates passed the shot contract at **72 frames, 960×540, 24 fps**. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 59/100** (subject 4, motion 1, composition 3, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 74/100** (4, 4, 3, 3, 5). The traveler, room modules, gate, ability, and modifier props were previewed alone before full rendering. Encoded MP4 frames at the start, ability pickup, gate opening, and final state, plus both GIFs' final frames, were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_run.py): equal module footprints, distinct biome materials, three moss tufts, three crystal spires, paired gate panels, distinct modifiers, and exact keyed poses. The wide empty area above the room strip reduces composition; the symbolic modifiers and simplified props limit finish.
