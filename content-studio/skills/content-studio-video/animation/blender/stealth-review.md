# Stealth infiltration review

The [original Blender builder](examples/stealth_infiltration.py) stages one infiltration route: a quiet blue cover lane, exposed metal plates under a guard's search pool, a ladder, and an elevated objective. The [pinned upstream stealth direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/genre-stealth/SKILL.md) calls for gameplay-relevant shadows, noise materials, and vertical infiltration. The visual lane contrasts concealment with exposure; it does not simulate guard vision, acoustics, or stealth game mechanics.

The search pool and sound cues are visual symbols; the characters and architecture have simplified geometry.
