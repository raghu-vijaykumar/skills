# Guardian sculpt, retopology, and groom review

The original [builder](examples/guardian_craft.py) and [shot specification](examples/guardian_craft.json) show one **stylized static guardian bust** in three inspectable stages: a code-authored high-density surface at left, a lower-density quad cage at right, and a short hair-card groom over the cage. The [pinned sculpting](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/sculpting/SKILL.md), [retopology](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/retopology/SKILL.md), and [hair-groom](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/hair-groom/SKILL.md) guidance informed the surface hierarchy, duplicate high-poly reference, quad cage, and atlased clump flow.

Two complete Blender 5.3 outputs passed the 72-frame, 960×540, 24 fps shot contract. Their encoded opening, midpoint, and final frames and complete 24-frame, 480×270 GIFs were reviewed. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **static greyblock v1 41/100** (subject 2, motion 1, composition 3, finish 1, evidence 5) and **selected v2 73/100** (4, 3, 4, 3, 5). V2's camera approaches as its hair-card tips bend and settle. The simple facial form and graphic, layered groom limit its finish. These are editorial scores, not audience metrics or production approval.

The [saved-scene audit](scripts/audit_guardian_craft.py) confirms the preserved clean base, editable two-level Multires modifier and restrained cloud displacement, 8,960 evaluated high-poly faces versus 336 low-poly faces, an 85.7% quad low shell shrinkwrapped to a hidden high reference, and a separate visible cage display. It also checks a packed 256×256 RGBA four-strip atlas, 32 four-segment UV hair cards, the dithered alpha material, a scalp cap, and keyed tip bend at frames 1, 36, and 72. The smooth features are separate meshes; the quad statistic describes the head shell only.

This demonstrates a **headless code-authored surface workflow**, not hand brush sculpting. The cage is suitable for a static bust, not facial animation: it lacks deliberate eye and mouth loops. The texture is a compact procedural color and alpha atlas, not baked strand normals. The groom uses shape-key breeze rather than simulation. No high-to-low normal bake, deformation test, hair physics, or engine import is claimed.

Rebuild v2 from the repository root using the [headless shot command](README.md#rebuild-the-showcases) with `<name>` set to `guardian_craft`. For v1, copy the JSON to ignored `tmp/guardian-v1.json`, set `variant` to `1`, remove the `hair` object and its beat references, and pass that file to the same renderer. Run `blender --background --python scripts/audit_guardian_craft.py -- --blend <saved.blend> --out <report.json>` for each scene. The best [GIF](previews/guardian-craft.gif) is embedded inline in the Blender README; rejected outputs remain ignored in `tmp/`.
