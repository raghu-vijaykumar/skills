# Realistic product-visualization treatment review

The [realistic lamp builder](examples/realistic_lamp.py) is an original clamp-mounted steel task lamp on a wood desk. The [manufacturer reference](https://www.ikea.com/eg/en/p/tertial-work-lamp-white-00355722/) establishes useful real-world cues: a 17 cm shade, powder-coated steel arm, and ABS clamp parts. The model is not a replica. It uses a measured 0.17 m shade, meter-scale scene units, separate clamp jaws and screw, two linked arms with tension springs, a power cord, restrained PBR values, procedural wood grain and bump, a perspective product camera, and softbox lighting. The upper hinge adjusts while its light comes on. This demonstrates `realistic-style` for this authored product visualization, not general photogrammetry, AAA game assets, or engine-ready export.

The exact Blender 5.3 MP4 is 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps, with SHA-256 `e653ea8b2e33b9c44d3bb7eb9a2ff5dd74b368d13eb42c1d2e7f45d186586df9`. The selected [optimized GIF](previews/realistic-lamp.gif) is 480×270, 24 frames, SHA-256 `0c07435f1e7570595d8ffec174f3bc8b83f2565c0d42a157ff3b8c473ecee9ee`. The sole full-render candidate scores **78/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json); selection is provisional until a competing full render is scored. Bounded previews exposed a cropped clamp and flat wood. Framing, grain, hinges, and spring detail were revised before the full candidate render.

The exact encoded MP4 frames at 0.25, 1.5, and 2.75 seconds were visually inspected. The complete clamp, shade, notebook, and desk edge remain in frame; the arm changes angle and the task light becomes visible on the notebook. The simple paper and clean procedural surfaces still look somewhat illustrative. The score therefore gives finish 3/5. No high-to-low bake, photo texture set, LOD, scan cleanup, or game-engine import is claimed.

The saved `.blend` passed the [scale and material audit](scripts/audit_realistic.py): metric scale, 0.17 m shade, 0.07 m desk thickness, perspective camera, separate clamp and spring parts, plausible recorded metal/plastic/wood material values, procedural bump, and 0.37 radians of upper-hinge movement. The [pinned upstream realistic guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/realistic-style/SKILL.md) informed the scale, material, and reference checks; the scene code and assets are original.
