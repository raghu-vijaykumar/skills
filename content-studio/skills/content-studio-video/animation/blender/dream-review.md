# Dream playground review

The [original Blender builder](examples/dream_playground.py) stages oversized alphabet blocks, an empty toy chair, disconnected nursery walls, hanging lights, and an unsupported doorway in a pale play space. The [pinned upstream dream/weirdcore direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/dream-weirdcore-style/SKILL.md) informed the nostalgic objects, unclear geography, soft colors, and unresolved reveal; all meshes and animation are original. This is one authored dream scene, not a general surreal scene generator.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 68/100** (subject 4, motion 2, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `402c4959ae2ce3c433c9e6e6b5328edbcbe1eb15b5cf529ada48557b01d2e857`; its GIF SHA-256 is `af53015205b4541e1e0546289f2deba6d79a3d03ff898e4b9596f8265119843d`. The toy and door move too little for the animation to read.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/dream-playground.gif) scores higher because the giant block rises and tilts while the unsupported door opens into a blank purple recess. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `edb9dc2f33ebc7c6dafd2222a6fd42e3c9b5b10e218c6ec80aece323ba29400c`; its GIF SHA-256 is `1136c267ab0094e7e028d918dcbf67045633a511e99e4e538bd9f26317b37a0e`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. The rejected output remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The giant block and doorway were previewed separately before the full composition. Exact encoded opening, action, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_dream.py): oversized block and editable letter, empty chair, four disconnected walls, eight hanging lights, unsupported door, keyed motion, and delivery settings. Both full renders passed the shot contract and exact MP4 frame and size checks. The haze is an art-directed low-contrast palette; it is not simulated volumetric fog or bloom. The foreground C block is deliberately cropped by the close framing.
