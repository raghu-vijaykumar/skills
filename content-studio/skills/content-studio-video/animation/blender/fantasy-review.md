# Low-magic fantasy gate review

The [original Blender builder](examples/fantasy_gate.py) makes one coherent medieval low-magic world: a stone keep, twin towers, crenellations, oak drawbridge, iron details, moat, and wool heraldic banners. The [pinned upstream fantasy-worlds direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/fantasy-worlds/SKILL.md) calls for a consistent fantasy subgenre, honest materials, readable heraldry, and gated magic effects. This scene has no magic effects. It demonstrates a castle gate lane, not every fantasy subgenre.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 68/100** (subject 4, motion 2, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `0baa612133ac1a91b04b6d9460b55bf804258331626a1d370d354758830fb414`; its GIF SHA-256 is `145a3a1db4c6b09fc39335ad4f75c8c6c48847502344f3917995ce1441e37228`. Its bridge barely lowers during the shot.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/fantasy-gate.gif) scores higher because the bridge visibly lowers over the moat and reaches the bank while the gateway and heraldry remain readable. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `b10e5d0a77d3d208f91feb610c8b3bc8f3ba8b60cb0e9f1968167c66dcdca94a`; its GIF SHA-256 is `18f859518f3ed6cebee51c246710d796a90f60e4d2017943c574d8ecbfaade1a`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. The rejected output remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The bridge was previewed alone, then in the full keep composition. Exact encoded opening, middle, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_fantasy.py): two towers, 16 merlons, two banners, nine planks, a 2.75 m gateway wider than the 2.47 m bridge, 0.2 m bank overlap, and bridge rotation from -1.44 radians to 0 in the selected shot. Camera travel is modest and keeps the lowering action in frame. Both full renders passed shot-contract and exact encoded-frame checks. Stone and cloth are simplified, and this is a stylized art-direction example rather than a historically precise reconstruction.
