# Measured studio interior review

The original [builder](examples/archviz_studio.py) and [shot specification](examples/archviz_studio.json) implement one small interior walkthrough under the pinned upstream [archviz direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/archviz/SKILL.md). A 5.6 m × 5.6 m × 3.0 m room has a true 2.10 m × 1.40 m window opening, separate architecture/furniture/light/context collections, a 30 mm eye-height camera with straight verticals, cool window light, and a warm pendant. The finished variant adds wood, linen, rug, wall art, and muted exterior masses. This is a bounded studio-interior recipe; it does not claim CAD matching, physically accurate glass, photoreal furnishings, or a general real-estate renderer.

Both full Blender 5.3 candidates passed the 72-frame, 960×540, 24 fps shot contract and the saved-scene [structural audit](scripts/audit_archviz.py). The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 50/100** (subject 3, motion 1, composition 3, finish 2, evidence 5) and selected **v2 73/100** (4, 3, 4, 3, 5). V1 is a static, monochrome greybox. V2 moves the level camera 1.00 m sideways and 0.60 m inward, revealing the furnished space; its simplified materials limit the finish score.
