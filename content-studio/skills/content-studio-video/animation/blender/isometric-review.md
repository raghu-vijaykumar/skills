# Isometric animation treatment review

The [isometric lamp builder](examples/isometric_lamp.py) presents the desk lamp and notebook as a one-tile diorama. It uses a locked orthographic camera on equal x/y/z axes, a 0.8 m square tile with a center pivot, distinct top and edge materials, and soft key and cool fill. The lamp swings down and lights the notebook while the camera stays fixed. This is a presentation and kit-framing style, distinct from the low-poly mesh and voxel-grid treatments. It demonstrates `isometric-style` for this authored lamp; other subjects and tile kits still need their own builders.

The exact Blender 5.3 MP4 is 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps, with SHA-256 `50c18c9afc8baebfef8402781b4ff59ddd0517ae85c744fdf66306a6d0535950`. The selected [optimized GIF](previews/isometric-lamp.gif) is 480×270, 24 frames, SHA-256 `8b477e9307b89383aea2ef1ca6aabc9b7e7af38fd449dd2e0fb297b0645c4f87`. The sole full-render candidate scores **87/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json); selection is provisional until a competing full render is scored. Bounded previews showed a cropped front tile edge. The camera framing was widened before the full candidate render.

The exact encoded MP4 frames at 0.25, 1.5, and 2.75 seconds were visually inspected. The entire tile, lamp, and notebook stay in frame; the upper arm and shade move while the warm light pool appears and holds. The notebook markings are simple, and this example does not demonstrate a dense city-builder tile set or game engine import.

The saved `.blend` passed the [isometric structural audit](scripts/audit_isometric.py): orthographic camera with equal absolute direction components (about 0.57735 each), camera locked for the kit, a center-pivot 1×1 tile of 0.8 m, different top and edge materials, and 0.53 radians of lamp-pivot movement across the three review frames. The [pinned upstream isometric guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/isometric-style/SKILL.md) informed camera, tile, and readability rules; the scene code and assets are original. The audit report, MP4, editable `.blend`, and frame cache remain in ignored `tmp/`.
