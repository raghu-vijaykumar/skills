# Minimalist pendulum review

The [original pendulum builder](examples/minimal_pendulum.py) uses a base, support, beam, rod, and one red bob against a warm bone stage. The graphite frame sits left of center so the right half remains intentional negative space. Only three named materials are used. Five keyed angles drive a diminishing swing. This demonstrates one authored minimalist kinetic sculpture, not a general abstract composition or physics simulator.

Both Blender 5.3 full renders are 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps and were scored in the [scorebook](scores.json).
