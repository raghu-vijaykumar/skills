# Frutiger Aero dashboard review

The [original dashboard builder](examples/aero_dashboard.py) combines a beveled aqua UI card, glossy bubble with a layered transmission/coat material, green leaf, white clouds, spring-green ground, and soft blue/white lighting. Three inset pill controls make the surface read as mid-2000s skeuomorphic UI. The bubble floats, a small trail rises, and a soft key moves across the panel. This is one authored nature-tech animation, not a functional interface or a general Frutiger Aero generator.

Two full Blender 5.3 outputs contain 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records v1 at **73/100** (subject 4, motion 3, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and v2 at **82/100** (4, 4, 4, 4, 5). Both full renders used resumable 48-frame batches and passed exact MP4 frame and resolution checks.
