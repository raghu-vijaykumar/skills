# Anime heroine pose review

The [original heroine builder](examples/anime_heroine.py) makes a compact bust with enlarged eyes, angular navy hair masses, a cobalt coat, red scarf, three hard value bands per cel material, 2 px Freestyle contours, and one graphic backdrop disc. Head, arm, and scarf have independent keyed controls. The arm lifts while the eyes blink at frame 36, then holds the pose. This demonstrates `anime-style` for this authored heroine, not a generic anime character generator or production rig.

Two full Blender 5.3 MP4 candidates were scored. Both are 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. Candidate v1 has SHA-256 `71824d09cd164260922334efb3d02aaae8ec22ed924524a8cdb5e7470a931a8e` and scored **74/100**: its held raised arm showed a shoulder gap. The corrected v2 has SHA-256 `37ec66e02e3a3de0e15c24033dae59df10c4f5fc9e704d7b8dfe0fe60f21768c` and scored **78/100**. The [selected optimized GIF](previews/anime-heroine.gif) is 480×270, 24 frames, SHA-256 `9a9041db8ad2f6cd5d4d60e6a11cba5bc922cb3a5e32e67c136bf1016148580b`. The rejected GIF is retained in ignored `tmp/`; both candidates and their five component scores are recorded in [scores.json](scores.json).

Exact encoded first, blink, and held frames were inspected. The second render keeps the hand and sleeve within the frame and closes the shoulder gap. The character remains deliberately simple: the bust has no full-body anatomy, lip sync, or deformation rig, and its face and hair do not reach a polished anime production finish. Its 3/5 finish score reflects that limit.

The saved v2 `.blend` passed the [anime structural audit](scripts/audit_anime.py): six three-step constant cel materials, an orthographic camera, 2 px Freestyle contour, eye width near half the head width, and independent animated head, arm, and scarf controls. The audit records a compressed eye scale at frame 36 and a lifted arm by frame 72. The [pinned upstream anime guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/anime-style/SKILL.md) informed the bands, silhouettes, face proportions, and limited palette; geometry and animation are original. The MP4s, editable `.blend` files, audits, frame caches, and rejected GIF remain in ignored `tmp/`.
