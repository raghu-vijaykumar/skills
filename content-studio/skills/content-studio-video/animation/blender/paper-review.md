# Stop-motion paper butterfly review

The [original butterfly builder](examples/paper_butterfly.py) stages a cut-paper puppet above a gridded craft mat. Six uneven wing pieces have physical thickness and packed 128×128 fiber textures. Fold seams, a rolled body seam, two fasteners, and masking tape make the assembly legible. A warm practical light, packed paper backdrop, and tracked table shadow support the handmade look. The wings and puppet move through held poses, including open and folded silhouettes. This is one authored paper-craft shot, not a general claymation or stop-motion generator.

Both full Blender 5.3 outputs were scored in the [scorebook](scores.json).
