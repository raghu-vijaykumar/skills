# Material, lookdev, and lighting lab review

The [original Blender builder](examples/material_light_lab.py) compares three equal forms under one controlled studio rig. A neutral clay pass establishes the geometry and values. The beauty pass assigns reusable polished metal, brushed metal, and glazed ceramic masters, then moves the warm key while fill and rim remain fixed. The pinned upstream [materials](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/materials/SKILL.md), [lookdev](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/lookdev/SKILL.md), and [lighting](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/lighting/SKILL.md) guidance informed the shared benchmark.
