# Chibi postal courier review

The [original courier builder](examples/chibi_courier.py) shows one full-body chibi character hopping and lifting a sealed letter. The head is 1.44 m of a 2.42 m figure, or **59.5%** of its height; the arms, legs, boots, and hands are deliberately small. A separate head control, offer arm, blink keys, and narrowing contact shadow make the motion readable. This demonstrates an authored super-deformed character shot, not a general character generator or rigging system.

Both Blender 5.3 full renders are 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps and were scored in the [scorebook](scores.json). Candidate v1 MP4 has SHA-256 `72ad1b840fb1f329b48de57eff7fc02c1c96030f58e0065406ae369f2d75a5eb`; its 480×270 24-frame GIF has SHA-256 `fb3c8cc57c0856a4a4a103096383ce5963e8bb1f0d2445d5b4c70b18a0faae5a`. It scored **70/100**: subject 4, motion 4, composition 3, finish 2, evidence 5. The figure and hop read, but the plain wall and single envelope provided little postal identity.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/chibi-courier.gif) scored **78/100**: subject 4, motion 4, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `6bbac99ab9bac058cf25fd8fdc6f575db37a387caf06d0cc8643a30d081cf763`; the GIF SHA-256 is `6fdccf74cfd27ec93d9f40bff1eb0f45967c5be66bf02d4938ceed7900b79a3a`. The cap badge, diagonal mail strap, side satchel, and roundel improve the story and stage. The rejected GIF and both full MP4s remain in ignored `tmp/`.

Exact encoded first, airborne, and landing frames and three sampled frames from the selected GIF were inspected. The huge head stays within the frame at the highest hop, while the letter rotates up and the feet clear the floor. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [chibi structural audit](scripts/audit_chibi.py): 0.595 head-to-height ratio, two short legs, parented head and arm controls, an editable letter, fixed orthographic camera, blink, 0.78 m hop, 1.14-radian letter offer, and contracted airborne shadow. The selected scene additionally retains the cap, satchel, and backdrop roundel. Simple facial surfaces and a restrained stage limit finish to 3/5; the GIF compresses fine facial detail.

The [pinned upstream chibi guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/chibi-style/SKILL.md) calls for a half-to-third-height head and simplified anatomy. The character, envelope, stage, and animation here are original.
