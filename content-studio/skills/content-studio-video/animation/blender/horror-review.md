# Psychological horror corridor review

The [horror corridor builder](examples/horror_corridor.py) is an original institutional set. It differs from the brighter [atmospheric corridor](review.md) through a 1.44 m path, 2.3 m ceiling, 1.6 m eye-height camera, a single wall-facing chair, one flickering fluorescent practical, a low-density green fog volume, and a door that slowly opens onto a warm slit. The geometry stays sparse. This demonstrates `horror-style` for this authored corridor; it does not establish every horror pack or interactive level design.

The exact Blender 5.3 MP4 is 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps, with SHA-256 `2bd19905220dc8ef77983a88bb809a94cddbec5edd6f8df8d329c5229084d495`. The selected [optimized GIF](previews/horror-corridor.gif) is 480×270, 24 frames, SHA-256 `2ab2408df3677333ac952302d016d9679208a6f64c872e9dffa7953548aec2c8`. The sole full-render candidate scores **77/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json); selection is provisional until a competing full render is scored. Bounded previews exposed a door slit that was visible too early and overly dense fog. Both were revised before the full candidate render.

The exact encoded MP4 frames at 0.25, 1.33, and 2.75 seconds were visually inspected. The practical falls nearly dark at the first flicker, the slow camera move increases pressure toward the lone chair, and the door opens enough to reveal the warm slit. The fast flicker is less obvious in the 8 fps GIF, so motion readability is scored 3/5. The route has no soundtrack, player collision, AI, or navigable 90° blind corner.

The saved `.blend` passed the [horror structural audit](scripts/audit_horror.py): 1.44 m hallway, 2.3 m ceiling, 1.6 m camera height, one narrative chair, fog density 0.027, roughly 9.6:1 practical-to-warm-light ratio, two near-black flicker frames, a 1.35 m camera push, and a 0.55 radian door opening. The [pinned upstream horror guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/horror-style/SKILL.md) informed the proportions, lighting, fog, and sparse prop placement; the code and assets are original. The audit report, MP4, editable `.blend`, and frame cache remain in ignored `tmp/`.
