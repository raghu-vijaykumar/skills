# Manga gaze panel review

The [original eye builder](examples/manga_gaze.py) makes a grayscale character close-up inside a framed print panel. A sparse packed screentone, dark fringe, large eye, timed ink lines, and exclamation cue distinguish this treatment from the full-color [anime hero](anime-review.md) and [comic rocket](comic-review.md). The iris shifts and dilates, then settles. This proves one authored `manga-style` shot, not a general manga conversion or character animation system.

The exact Blender 5.3 MP4 is 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps, SHA-256 `dfdd28164bf11005d5841aa927bfbd2fce419206e4b6c182e0c9965271ef1640`. The selected [optimized GIF](previews/manga-gaze.gif) is 480×270, 24 frames, SHA-256 `7cfffa47a26aff9ef7dc856800fdba265b6b25872e18b7e6111972654f4533c8`. The sole full-render candidate scores **78/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json); selection is provisional until a competing full render is scored. Bounded previews established the eye crop, screentone scale, and ink timing before the full candidate render.

Exact encoded first, action, and held frames were inspected. A sampled GIF strip retains the iris movement, dilation, seven emphasis strokes, and `!` at 8 fps. The large eye and split print texture remain readable at README size. The facial drawing is deliberately spare, and the mechanically simple hair and cheek shapes limit finish to 3/5. Subject fidelity, motion, and composition score 4/5; the traceable exact render and scene audit score evidence integrity 5/5.

The saved `.blend` passed the [manga structural audit](scripts/audit_manga.py): 2.1 px ink outline, five grayscale materials, packed 256×256 screentone, seven emphasis lines, four border bars, 1.4× close-up face scale, iris movement from world x 0.886 m at frame 1 to 1.528 m at frame 36, iris scale change from 0.83× to 1.30×, and the exclamation hidden at frame 1 but visible by frame 36. The [pinned upstream manga guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/manga-style/SKILL.md) informed the grayscale, screentone, and emphasis choices; the panel and animation are original. The MP4, editable `.blend`, audit, and frame cache remain in ignored `tmp/`.
