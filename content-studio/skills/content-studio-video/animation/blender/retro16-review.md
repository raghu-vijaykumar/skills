# Retro 16-bit fox review

The [original fox builder](examples/retro_16bit_fox.py) crosses a dithered mountain and waterfall landscape. A packed 160×120 nearest-neighbor texture supplies 12 scene colors; square-pixel fox and water meshes add three more, using a 16-entry authored palette overall. A locked 320×240 camera, 12 fps, emission-only sprite fills, and held position keys keep texels crisp. The checker and stripe patterns use explicit pixel colors rather than smooth gradients. This is one authored side-scrolling shot, not a general 16-bit animation generator.
