# Sunlit folk rite review

The [original Blender builder](examples/folk_ritual.py) places a handmade wicker sun in a ring of ten marked stones and six crimson offerings. A grassy field, rounded hills, trees, and four closed huts keep the setting pastoral and village scale. The [pinned upstream folk horror direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/folk-horror-style/SKILL.md) informed the warm daylight, handmade ritual props, and repeating symbols; the scene geometry and motion are original. This one authored rite does not constitute a general folk horror generator.
