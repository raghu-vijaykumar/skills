# Stylized windmill island review

The [original windmill builder](examples/stylized_windmill.py) makes a storybook island with an oversized triangular roof, round puff tree, stable cottage body, four-color sage/cream/terracotta/warm direction, matte surfaces, a packed 64×64 painted roof albedo, and light contour lines. The windmill turns 3.95 radians in three seconds while the window changes from dark teal to warm light. This demonstrates `stylized-style` for one authored windmill island. It is distinct from the anime character treatment and is not a generic stylization filter for arbitrary assets.

Two full Blender 5.3 MP4 candidates were scored. Both are 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. Candidate v1 has SHA-256 `6f4ac69349db20b3294f715d6f7b247335f4f4f54501de1f99bdc9b642be6646` and scored **78/100**; its island base touched the lower frame edge. Candidate v2 widens the camera, has SHA-256 `b7a48d47da3abce71e5e031a13dcbdd37cdfcdab884604d9bd577d79dfb0ecc5`, and scored **82/100**. The [selected optimized GIF](previews/stylized-windmill.gif) is 480×270, 24 frames, SHA-256 `9b67db3fcef052ee8d7da8d1d05e25f9bb4324935aaf0d57b43b8e2ab3ff0ab6`. The rejected GIF remains in ignored `tmp/`; the [scorebook](scores.json) records both candidates and all five editorial scores.

Exact encoded first, action, and held frames were inspected. A sampled GIF strip confirms that the windmill rotation and window state survive 8 fps and the reduced size. The fully framed v2 island remains a simple diorama: its roof paint is broad color variation rather than production hand painting, the tree is stylized rather than botanical, and the camera and background are static. Those limits keep subject fidelity, motion, composition, and finish at 4/5.

The saved v2 `.blend` passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_stylized.py): roof-to-body radius 1.81, canopy-to-trunk width 6.29, declared four-color direction, packed 64×64 roof albedo, nine matte materials, 1.5 px Freestyle contour, 3.95-radian windmill turn, and window emission increasing from 0 to 2.5. The [pinned upstream stylized guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/stylized-style/SKILL.md), [NPR reference](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/stylized-style/references/npr-shaders.md), and [palette reference](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/stylized-style/references/palette-examples.md) informed those choices; the scene and texture are original. MP4s, editable `.blend` files, audits, and frame caches remain in ignored `tmp/`.
