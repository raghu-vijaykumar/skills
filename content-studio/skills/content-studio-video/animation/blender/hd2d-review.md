# HD-2D lantern bridge review

The [original builder](examples/hd2d_bridge.py) puts a square-pixel lantern carrier on a physically shaded stone bridge over a stream. A packed 128×64 painted deck albedo and softened 3D trees keep the stage distinct from the emission-only billboard actor. Two warm keys and a cool fill light the stage; a tilted orthographic camera focuses the bridge with depth of field. The pixel actor stays editable as separate color meshes and two held foot poses. This is one authored HD-2D shot, not a general pixel-to-3D converter.

Three full Blender 5.3 outputs are 72 frames at 960×540, 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records v1 at **73/100** (subject 4, motion 3, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5), v2 at **78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5), and v3 at **82/100** (4, 4, 4, 4, 5). One eye, a hat band, cloak trim, and a brighter lantern accent make the actor read at full-shot size. Each GIF is a 24-frame 480×270 summary; all MP4s and rejected GIFs remain in ignored `tmp/`.

The carrier was reviewed alone in a 480×480 isolated frame, then in the bridge composition. Exact encoded opening, crossing, and held frames were inspected for every full candidate; three selected-GIF poses were checked after quantization. All three saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_hd2d.py): square pixel meshes, packed painted albedo, 5.15 m 3D bridge, warm/cool light rig, tilted camera with DOF, held walk, and the candidate-specific reeds and ripples. The three full renders used the resumable 48-frame batch path and passed exact MP4 frame and resolution validation. The [pinned upstream HD-2D guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/hd-2d-style/SKILL.md) informed the pixel actor, 3D stage, warm keys, and DOF; all geometry, textures, and animation are original. The shot does not include volumetric god rays or a narrated composition.
