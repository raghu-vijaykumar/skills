# Voxel animation treatment review

The [voxel lamp builder](examples/voxel_lamp.py) reuses the desk-lamp subject so the visual treatment can be compared with the measured [prop](lamp-review.md) and [low-poly variant](lowpoly-review.md). It builds an explicit 0.05 m occupancy grid and emits only exposed cube faces. A stepped silhouette, five-color palette, flat axis-aligned faces, and whole-voxel head movement distinguish this recipe from the irregular faceted low-poly mesh. The task light switches on over a simple grid-built notebook. This proves the `voxel-style` route for this authored lamp, not arbitrary voxel subjects.

The exact Blender 5.3 MP4 is 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps, with SHA-256 `9e2a0e78ce8b7db7e29effb6c908aff9807d2e9bdde7a2296d0b1229fb78eae6`. The selected [optimized GIF](previews/voxel-lamp.gif) is 480×270, 24 frames, SHA-256 `bff9bab3c1aafe29d1fea4a7a5ab41ae1e9b6b82045b3549c0622b04d55f5c66`. The sole full-render candidate scores **87/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json); selection is provisional until a competing full render is scored. An early bounded preview showed a gap where a horizontal and vertical pivot step happened together. The pivot was changed to vertical whole-voxel steps before the full candidate render.

The exact encoded MP4 frames at 0.25, 1.5, and 2.75 seconds were visually inspected. The head stays connected to the elbow, lowers in two discrete grid steps, and makes a warm pool on the notebook. The lamp remains fully framed. The notebook has no page details, and the head movement is deliberately abrupt rather than smooth.

The saved `.blend` passed the [voxel structural audit](scripts/audit_voxel.py): 162 occupied cells across seven separately editable prop meshes, 0.05 m grid placement, five prop materials, flat axis-aligned faces, zero modifiers, and three grid-snapped head positions checked over all 72 source frames. The [pinned upstream voxel guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/voxel-style/SKILL.md) informed the grid, palette, and modular shape rules; the scene code and assets are original. The audit report, exact MP4, editable `.blend`, and frame cache remain in ignored `tmp/`.
