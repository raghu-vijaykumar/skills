# Cozy reading nook review

The [original Blender builder](examples/cozy_nook.py) chooses one cozy lane: a small reading nook with a rounded sage chair, coral throw, warm window, book shelves, tea table, rug, and contained plant. The [pinned upstream cozy-wholesome direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/cozy-wholesome-mood/SKILL.md) asks for warm keys and soft fill, rounded props, safe clutter, gentle palettes, and a friendly eye-height camera. These are applied to this scene rather than treated as a general cozy renderer.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 68/100** (subject 4, motion 2, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `560284715f7dd53b9f75fa30e474356b4e9b7e5d146242e7b75fdeb279f57023`; its GIF SHA-256 is `fc27b7637485b246e3db1d40a7c669d58ffc6288140918011b4c1d6916d2a6b5`. The gentle room dressing reads, but chair motion and camera movement are minimal.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/cozy-nook.gif) scores higher because the chair rocks through two clearly different poses while the lamp warms and the camera moves toward the reading area. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `cc56f102c3e878135b9022cc985edde7a6608302c6ee73250912522132c65c1b`; its GIF SHA-256 is `c21f782ecf46ffb49d078586e4dd9ad7d66dee9c1d434b4b7f13eba0ac51dfe4`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. The rejected output remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The chair was previewed alone at its opening, forward, and backward rock poses, then inspected in the room. Exact encoded first, action, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_cozy.py): 11 attached chair parts with a beveled seat, seven plant leaves, ten shelf books, a rug and tea mug, warm soft key and fill, and a keyed eye-height camera. In v2 the chair moves from 0 to +0.085 to -0.078 radians, the practical lamp rises from 90 to 460 watts, bulb emission from 0.15 to 3, and the camera advances 1.2 m. Both full renders passed shot-contract and encoded-frame checks. The room is deliberately stylized, and its straight rocker rails and simple books are illustrative rather than production furniture detail.
