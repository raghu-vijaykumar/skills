# Mascot puppet show review

The [original Blender builder](examples/puppet_stage.py) puts a round felt character on a bright children's-show set with pink curtains, a friendly brand banner, a stitched smile, and hard plastic eyes. The [pinned upstream puppet horror direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/mascot-puppet-horror/SKILL.md) informed the craft surfaces, cheerful language, mismatched gaze, and sick color shift. The character and animation are original. This is one authored shot, not a general puppet or costume animation system.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 72/100** (subject 4, motion 2, composition 4, finish 4, evidence 5) and **v2 82/100** (4, 4, 4, 4, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `10b754eee71bf6e14cc344ba8720dc71d4933f7ba320a8fbd2d13fc3a89545f0`; its GIF SHA-256 is `82bcad9df6cea96641fd8ec7218bf821fb0ecae3763035d2f428c6406c7311bf`. Its tiny body turn and camera drift do not make the performance uncanny.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/puppet-stage.gif) scores higher because one pupil slides out of alignment, the warm stage key dims, a green light rises, and the character tilts while its stitched smile stays fixed. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `7d9727e79ef39c9d0fff8bd1250b1b4950c1469838b7f5efc4affd2fec922ce9`; its GIF SHA-256 is `b1d13f4443b80e72e8b83efe3750c4155cd52149390b20034350b4ea77f411c7`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. The rejected candidate remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The felt figure was previewed alone and then on the full set. Exact encoded opening, middle, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_puppet.py): felt bump material, two plastic eyes, nine smile stitches, a show banner, curtains, camera motion, and the v2 gaze and light cues. Both full renders passed the shot contract and exact MP4 frame and size checks. Procedural bump only suggests felt fibers, and the character is a simple keyed model rather than a physical puppet or cloth simulation. The camera push crops the banner at the end to prioritize the face.
