# Brutalist archive gateway review

The [original Blender builder](examples/brutalist_archive.py) chooses one austere monumental lane for `brutalist-mood`: two 10.7 m board-formed concrete monoliths, an offset cantilever, a sliding concrete shutter, a dark recessed opening, an almost empty slab plaza, and one 1.76 m amber witness. The [pinned upstream brutalist guide](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/brutalist-mood/SKILL.md) calls for monumental concrete, material restraint, deep shadow, and a human scale cue. This is one stylized architecture scene, not a general architectural renderer.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates passed the shot contract at **72 frames, 960×540, 24 fps**. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 63/100** (subject 4, motion 1, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `76552c3c6126d06a4009ea248b06958313ba667ed3be68d1cb8d3b174cf1a578`; its GIF SHA-256 is `6715291680b53429dfbb76f7945d30baa18218ec34fb64527950b831ab93a169`. Its gateway stays nearly closed, so the opening reads poorly despite a strong massing frame.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/brutalist-archive.gif) shows the shutter moving 4.2 m on visible tracks, exposing the dark void while the witness advances 4.05 m and the low camera pushes in 2.1 m. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `28027b597fdeae0d565ba627b0020a13eed294e0b17334917b3288e8cd81d55b`; its GIF SHA-256 is `07871ca0f210715b8c3e77ed1b949eb02b087eeeb09d1856844c3b13bf81af82`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. The rejected output remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The gateway and shutter were previewed alone before full rendering. Encoded opening, action, and held MP4 frames and each GIF's final frame were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_brutalist.py): monolith-to-witness height ratio above 6, 14 board seams, eight tie holes, five used materials, concrete roughness above 0.9, restrained fill, a real sliding rig, and exact keyed poses. The witness translates without an articulated gait, and the concrete remains deliberately simplified. The clip demonstrates scene and motion direction, not building feasibility or construction detail.
