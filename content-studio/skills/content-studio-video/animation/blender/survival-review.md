# Survival shelter review

The [original Blender builder](examples/survival_shelter.py) stages a warm refuge beside a cold hazard lane. Wood, a water canteen, steel scraps, and a hammer identify the crafting bench; a four-wheel salvage cart returns across a bright safety boundary, a small hearth lights, and a red hazard watcher retreats. The [pinned upstream survival direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/genre-survival/SKILL.md) asks for resources readable at speed, safe and unsafe zones, and an iconic crafting bench. The scene communicates those cues symbolically; it does not implement survival gameplay, inventory transfer, or a craft system.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates passed the shot contract at **72 frames, 960×540, 24 fps**. The fire is a simplified graphic cone and the resources are static props; the score reflects that finish limit.
