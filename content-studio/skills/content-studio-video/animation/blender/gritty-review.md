# Dark freight depot review

The [original Blender builder](examples/gritty_depot.py) chooses one dark industrial lane: a vacant freight depot with a heavy gantry, oxidized columns, stained concrete, a patched shutter, old barrels, and a solitary suspended cargo cage. The [pinned upstream dark-gritty direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/dark-gritty-mood/SKILL.md) calls for wear as story, low-key light, melancholy color, and industrial weight. The scene applies those cues to a single freight bay; it does not claim every dark or gritty setting.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 63/100** (subject 4, motion 1, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `fadd5d927f4b7b797f68f7f3ddd3a7cc8098eaeee3115d833472b74f3a0b8ecf`; its GIF SHA-256 is `8a6baf286db050ccb7f2b3bc28cc9c37ac225d27c571a4eabbd56814845b9133`. V1 retains the mood, but its load barely moves.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/gritty-depot.gif) scores higher because the crane load moves more than four meters along the gantry with restrained swing as the camera advances. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `13335530ab54e141323248fcc02020033f7922532b29feebacec87a22afa53cd`; its GIF SHA-256 is `e562961c9ca552e565eabbb1cb0762385f5cecad2df8fb1503571003e8062dc6`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. The rejected output remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The cage, hook, and chain were previewed alone before full-shot inspection. The first preview exposed a short chain and a trolley that detached from the gantry during the last drop; both were corrected before full rendering. Exact encoded opening, action, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_gritty.py): 11 chain links, five overhead trusses, ten uprights, nine paint chips, five rust drips, an oxidized noise-and-ramp material, and cold shaft versus lone warm practical lighting. Both full renders passed shot-contract and encoded-frame checks. The cage attachment and weathering are illustrative stylized geometry rather than an engineering load calculation or photo-accurate decay.
