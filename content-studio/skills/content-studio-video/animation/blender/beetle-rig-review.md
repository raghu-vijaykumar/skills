# Reed beetle creature and rig review

The original [builder](examples/beetle_rig.py) and [shot specification](examples/beetle_rig.json) demonstrate one **stylized insectoid creature and rigid-shell FK rig**. Six two-part legs, a separate head, thorax, abdomen, paired eyes and antennae, and two elytra establish an arthropod silhouette. The [pinned creature direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/creature-artist/SKILL.md) informed its segmented movement logic; the [rigging direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/rigging/SKILL.md) informed named bone hierarchy, weight binding, and pose tests. It does not demonstrate organic skin deformation, IK, facial controls, a generalized quadruped rig, or engine import.

Two full Blender 5.3 animations passed the version 2 72-frame, 960×540, 24 fps contract. Their encoded first, midpoint, and final frames and the complete 24-frame, 480×270 GIFs were reviewed. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **static greyblock v1 54/100** (subject 3, motion 1, composition 4, finish 2, evidence 5) and **selected v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). The first has no animated rig action. The second reveals both wing covers and changes alternating leg poses while retaining a clear three-quarter silhouette. Its simplified, toy-like surface limits finish to 3/5. Scores are editorial observations, not audience measurements or production approval.

The [saved-scene audit](scripts/audit_beetle_rig.py) verifies an 18-bone armature with root, thorax, head, abdomen, symmetric wing bones, and six upper/lower leg chains. All 27 creature meshes have one armature modifier and complete rigid weights on a named bone. Both elytra have thickness, and the scene includes a metric stage and fixed orthographic camera. The finished rig keys wing angles from 0 to 0.62 radians and back, with opposite-phase leg movement. The exact encoded midpoint shows both wing covers raised. Rigid weights fit the segmented exoskeleton; this is not a soft-tissue deformation or production facial rig.

Rebuild v2 from the repository root using the [headless shot command](README.md#rebuild-the-showcases) with `<name>` set to `beetle_rig`. For v1, copy the JSON to ignored `tmp/beetle-v1.json`, set `variant` to `1`, and pass that file as `--input` to the same renderer with a distinct MP4 output. Run `blender --background --python scripts/audit_beetle_rig.py -- --blend <saved.blend> --out <report.json>` from this directory for each saved scene. The best [GIF](previews/beetle-rig.gif) is embedded inline in the root README; rejected full media remain ignored in `tmp/`.
