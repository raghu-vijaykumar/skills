# Underwater kelp biome review

The [original Blender builder](examples/kelp_forest.py) chooses one biome lane: a blue underwater kelp field with silt, algae-worn rocks, sparse branching coral, a bubble column, and a schooling group of fish. The [pinned upstream biome-worlds direction](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/biome-worlds/SKILL.md) ties palette, fog, vegetation density, and material wear to climate. The blue filtered-light palette, 24 kelp stands, and algae rock material establish the chosen underwater lane. This does not claim arctic, desert, jungle, cave, or floating-island examples, and volumetric underwater haze is not simulated.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 68/100** (subject 4, motion 2, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `2e10ef842c624af4f65e1ef058d217ba9ddf84f518fea4e4e4c12153a5de3041`; its GIF SHA-256 is `af5a571ffb78806b7f03fbae9bd04ba886fae9ea0b48fa70632ea9555aa96a29`. The fish and bubbles barely move and kelp holds still in v1.

The [selected v2 GIF](previews/kelp-forest.gif) scores higher because seven amber fish cross the kelp field while bubbles rise, blades sway, and the camera glides forward. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `e650345132f311f062d068d46ffda441939dad5435557cbf1cb62d0732b401b2`; its GIF SHA-256 is `9bb3cd22b144fbb1d76c41d8a9664073a73064d876bdb3749daeb0bd9adce45e`. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. The rejected output remains in ignored `tmp/`.

The school was previewed alone, then in the full biome. Its first isolated preview occluded three of seven fish; a two-row arrangement and warmer fish material made every fish readable before full rendering. Exact encoded opening, middle, and final MP4 frames and both GIFs' final poses were inspected. Both saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_kelp.py): 24 kelp rigs and 72 segments, 14 algae-worn rocks, 11 coral stalks, seven fish with eyes, 17 bubbles, and filtered blue-green lights. In v2 the school moves from x=-3.8 m to x=1.55 m, bubbles rise 1.15 m, kelp bends, and the camera advances. Both full renders passed shot-contract and exact encoded-frame checks. The vegetation is deliberately simple stylized geometry, and the water has no fluid or volumetric simulation.
