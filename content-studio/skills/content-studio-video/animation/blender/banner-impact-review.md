# Banner cloth, rigid impact, and energy FX review

The original [builder](examples/banner_impact.py) and [shot specification](examples/banner_impact.json) show three bounded technical routes in one stylized prop shot. A pinned fabric banner reacts to wind, a copper orb falls onto a passive conductive plate, and twelve emissive mesh ribbons plus a ring mark the impact. The [pinned cloth](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/cloth-sim/SKILL.md), [physics](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/physics-sim/SKILL.md), and [VFX](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/vfx-fx/SKILL.md) guidance informed its pinning, pre-roll, active/passive colliders, baked delivery, and game-style mesh effect.

Two complete Blender 5.3 outputs passed the 72-frame, 960×540, 24 fps shot contract. Both exact MP4s and 24-frame, 480×270 GIFs decoded; their opening, impact, midpoint, and held frames were inspected. The finished banner and impact were also previewed alone. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **static v1 41/100** (subject 2, motion 1, composition 3, finish 1, evidence 5) and **selected v2 74/100** (4, 4, 4, 2, 5). In v2, the orb reaches the plate at frame 17 and the energy burst peaks one frame later. The cloth folds edge-on around its mast and the simple graphic FX has no volume, so finish stays at 2/5. Scores are editorial observations, not audience measurements or production approval.

The [saved-scene audit](scripts/audit_banner_impact.py) verifies a 117-vertex quad cloth source with a nine-vertex pin column, separate mast collider, 20-frame simulation pre-roll, and 72 delivered cloth shape keys. Blender's Cloth modifier is preserved but disabled for playback; the evaluated poses were baked to the visible mesh so frame batches do not depend on an in-memory simulation cache. The audit also checks the original active sphere and passive box colliders, a 120-substep rigid world, 72 baked orb transforms, the recorded impact frame, bounded ribbons, emissive materials, and effect start and tail. The wind source is disabled after baking. These saved source objects retain the settings used to generate the delivery keys.

The cloth example is a flag-like banner, **not a tested garment or character collider**. The rigid-body lane covers one sphere and plate, not fracture, joints, soft bodies, or an engine physics runtime. The VFX lane is an authored real-time mesh effect, not Mantaflow smoke/fire or particle simulation. No external Alembic/USD cache or engine flipbook export is claimed. A real production shot still needs its approved plan and exact-master creative review.

Rebuild v2 from the repository root using the [headless shot command](README.md#rebuild-the-showcases) with `<name>` set to `banner_impact`. For v1, copy the JSON to ignored `tmp/banner-v1.json`, set `variant` to `1`, and remove the `fx` object and beat reference before rendering to a separate MP4. Run `blender --background --python scripts/audit_banner_impact.py -- --blend <saved.blend> --out <report.json>` for each scene. The best [GIF](previews/banner-impact.gif) is embedded inline in the Blender README; rejected full outputs remain ignored in `tmp/`.
