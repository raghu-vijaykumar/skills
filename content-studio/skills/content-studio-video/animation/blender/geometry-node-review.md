# Geometry Nodes walkway review

The [original builder](examples/geometry_node_walkway.py) assembles two rows of repeated posts from a reusable Geometry Nodes group, places stage, procedural kit, hero, and lights in named collections, and animates a drone through a retracting gate. This demonstrates the pinned upstream [procedural-modeling](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/procedural-modeling/SKILL.md), [geometry-nodes](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/geometry-nodes/SKILL.md), and [scene-assembly](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/scene-assembly/SKILL.md) directions in one bounded modular-set lane. It does not demonstrate linked libraries, collection overrides, terrain generation, or engine streaming.

Both complete Blender 5.3 candidates passed the 72-frame, 960×540, 24 fps shot contract. The [shared score record](scores.json) rates **v1 58/100** (subject 3, motion 1, composition 4, finish 3, evidence 5) and **v2 73/100** (4, 3, 4, 3, 5). Its four-post rows and static drone make a weak traversal. The generated post kit and drone were previewed alone. The first overview showed an unclear gate opening; the gate was changed to retract visibly before full rendering. Encoded MP4 frames at the start, travel, gate approach, and held result were inspected, as was the final GIF frame. Both GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. Both saved scenes passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_geometry_node_walkway.py): Mesh Line, Instance on Points, Realize Instances, and Set Material nodes; four versus ten posts per row; four collection boundaries; delivery settings; drone crossing; and gate retraction. The scene is a compact diorama and its post kit uses one shape and two materials.
