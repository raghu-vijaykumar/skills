# Low-poly animation treatment review

The [low-poly lamp builder](examples/lowpoly_lamp.py) uses the measured lamp subject as a comparison with the smooth [craft benchmark](lamp-review.md), while changing the geometry, material response, lighting, and camera recipe. It uses six-sided arms, an eight-sided shade and base, faceted joints, flat shading, four simple prop materials, a single graphic key, and an orthographic view. The head tilts and its task light switches on during the 4-second motion. It demonstrates the `lowpoly-style` route for this authored lamp; other subjects still require their own builder and review.

The exact Blender 5.3 MP4 is 96 frames at 1280×720 and 24 fps, with SHA-256 `22e49f9366a85d47917d2278dc1776978925b6517585194b8a27d999ff218951`. The selected [optimized GIF](previews/lowpoly-lamp.gif) is 480×270, 32 frames, SHA-256 `74cb5ccd7894aa61eeb1ec20d7f734a964c96433f733b6f7c8f88553fcb88b80`. The sole full-render candidate scores **87/100** in the [scorebook](scores.json); selection is provisional until a competing render is scored. The first preview cropped the top and base. The orthographic camera was widened before the full render, and that preview was never a full gallery candidate.

The exact encoded frames at 0.25, 2.0, and 3.75 seconds were visually inspected. The full silhouette and notebook stay in frame; faceted faces are readable at each state; the head lowers and a sharp warm light pool appears. This is a clean minimal look with no surface texture or game-engine PS1 effects.

The saved `.blend` passed [topology audit](scripts/audit_lowpoly.py): eight separately editable prop meshes, **184 source triangles** within the upstream 50–500 prop guideline, four prop materials, all flat shaded, zero bevel or subdivision modifiers, an orthographic camera, and an animated head pivot. The [pinned upstream low-poly guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/lowpoly-style/SKILL.md) informed the silhouette, budget, and simple-material choices; the scene code and assets are original. The audit report, MP4, editable `.blend`, and frame cache remain in ignored `tmp/`.
