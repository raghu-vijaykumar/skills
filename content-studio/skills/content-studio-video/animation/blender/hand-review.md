# Mutating hand review

The [original hand builder](examples/mutating_hand.py) shows almost-human anatomy changing into a sixth digit. Skin, surgical steel, and bone-white plastic share the palm while the hand opens under a cold key and red rim. The [pinned upstream body-horror guidance](https://github.com/arjun988/blender-skills/blob/904f095d8f36a6e72adaebb08d07f884a074743d/.claude/skills/body-horror-style/SKILL.md) informed recognition, mutation, and material transition; all forms and motion are original. This is a stylized, nongraphic hand shot, not a general sculpting or creature generator.

Three full Blender 5.3 renders have 72 frames at 960×540 and 24 fps. Four output artifacts were scored because v3 received two GIF encodings. The [scorebook](scores.json) records **v1 64/100** (subject 3, motion 3, composition 4, finish 2, evidence 5), **v2 73/100** (3, 4, 4, 3, 5), **v3 ordered-dither GIF 74/100** (4, 4, 4, 2, 5), and **v3 diffusion GIF 78/100** (4, 4, 4, 3, 5). V1's MP4 SHA-256 is `7a1122b6125f7df7cb660d419bab8025906897dfe83fc0e68c82d8bd56d911c4` and GIF SHA-256 is `952fc0023393c930fab00140179024fc5bf78cae2f1b18e166b06608df5c56ce`. V2's MP4 SHA-256 is `3afac14bc4d7872ceab9cab50cfa48afa5d40e338e313bb1a1d4e0351433f98b` and GIF SHA-256 is `6a6bdd1a69949fbb9fc493355ef84e0cb82a120b88330c6284174d3b4a6e1635`. Both have seven total digits: the first iteration missed the intended single extra digit.

V3 has four ordinary fingers, a thumb, and one growing metal and ceramic branch. Its MP4 SHA-256 is `405580b2cdc53544889ed6d462370d56d4e2d83528fa94f732d868ee19ec1e5d`. The ordered-dither GIF SHA-256 is `9983e5db5a268c4d91df009521ab4b71a0fae945120ba2e1a145d064a6ce7a24`; it has a distracting checked skin pattern. The [selected diffusion GIF](previews/mutating-hand.gif) is smoother and has SHA-256 `49f69624e454dc3606f3fd4d3bbbbc18daddf06674b1ecec7a88222eb1fe550c`. All four GIFs decode as 24 frames at 480×270. Rejected artifacts remain in ignored `tmp/`.

The hand was inspected alone before the staged shot. Exact encoded opening, middle, and final frames and the selected GIF were inspected. All three saved `.blend` files passed the [structural audit](scripts/audit_hand.py): recognized palm, individual animated fingers, extra branch, fused skin/metal/plastic, camera, and candidate-specific motion. Resumable full renders passed the shot contract and exact MP4 frame and size checks. The fingers and palm still have simplified procedural forms, which limits the finish and realism scores.
