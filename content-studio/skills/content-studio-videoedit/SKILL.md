# Content Studio Video Edit

This skill also owns the shared channel helper used by archive, ideagen, and preprocessing for backup, Drive sync, analytics, capture ordering, and preprocessing. Read [the channel helper](references/channel-helper.md) for those commands and their workspace conventions.

Read the channel rules and draft timeline. Drafts use a canonical layout: raw `original/`, internal `.content-studio/processed-clips/` and `.content-studio/rough-cut/`, plus `<id>-rough-cut.mp4`, `<id>-preprocessed.mp4`, `<id>-final.mp4`, `<id>-audio-only.mp3`, `title.txt`, and `description.txt` at the draft root. After preprocessing, use `content-studio-autocut` when you need a reviewable rough cut; continue here for trim, cut, join, and thumbnail. For a simple edit, use `python <this-skill>/scripts/content.py edit trim --input <clip> --start 00:12 --end 00:30 --out <new-clip>` or `edit cut --input <clip> --remove 00:12-00:14 --out <new-clip>`. `edit join --channel <slug> --draft <id>` assembles ordered clips, preferring approved cuts and processed outputs, and writes `<id>-final.mp4` plus `.content-studio/rough-cut/edit-timeline.json` and `edit-timeline.html`. For a standalone draft folder outside a channel workspace, use `edit join --folder <draft>`. For a curated sequence, repeat `--input <clip>` in the desired order and set `--out`. Review every transition and the full result. Manual editing remains available for complex assembly.

Edits are frame accurate. SDR and HLG cuts keep their respective color tags; Dolby Vision cuts require explicit `--sdr` because their RPU cannot survive a frame accurate re-encode. Each output path must be new. The automatic joined master is 1920x1080 BT.709 SDR at 30 fps, with HDR tone mapping and orientation-aware fitting. Use `--orientation landscape|portrait|square` to filter mismatched clips, `--include-mismatched` or `--allow <filename>` for intentional exceptions, and `--fit blur|pad|crop` to choose a blurred copy behind the clip (default), black padding, or crop-to-fill. Read the [edit guide](references/channel-helper.md) before using those commands.

Vlog drafts do not generate transcripts or captions. Before writing titles or descriptions, sample frames across the master and read any visible signs, locations, subjects, or activities; use supplied narration or voiceover when present. Never use placeholders such as "test edit" or "6 clip test edit". If the content is ambiguous, ask the user instead of inventing a subject. Read [the metadata workflow](references/metadata.md) for the required sampling and wording rules.

Capture 4-6 representative frames and the spoken or voiceover context. Use an image-generation model to create a **16:9 landscape AI thumbnail** that reflects the actual subject, setting, and hook. Do not use a raw frame with overlaid text as the final thumbnail unless the user explicitly asks. If there is no speech, say so and use ambient audio plus the visible subject; do not invent a spoken hook. Show the generated image for approval before writing `thumbnail.png`. Read [the AI thumbnail workflow](references/thumbnail.md) for the prompt and requirements. Choose background music with `content-studio-music`; it writes `music-selection.json` and `music-credits.txt` after approval. Present the master, title, description, thumbnail, and music credits together for review. Hand the approved package to `content-studio-publish`; publication still needs the user's explicit request.
