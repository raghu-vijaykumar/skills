# Content Studio Video Edit

This skill also owns the shared channel helper used by archive, ideagen, and preprocessing for backup, Drive sync, analytics, capture ordering, and preprocessing. Read [the channel helper](references/channel-helper.md) for those commands and their workspace conventions.

Read the channel rules and draft timeline. After preprocessing, use `content-studio-autocut` when you need a reviewable rough cut; continue here for trim, cut, join, transcript, captions, and thumbnail. For a simple edit, use `python <this-skill>/scripts/content.py edit trim --input <clip> --start 00:12 --end 00:30 --out <new-clip>` or `edit cut --input <clip> --remove 00:12-00:14 --out <new-clip>`. `edit join --channel <slug> --draft <id>` assembles ordered clips, preferring approved cuts and processed outputs. For a curated sequence, repeat `--input <clip>` in the desired order and set `--out`. Review every transition and the full result. Manual editing remains available for complex assembly.

Edits are frame accurate. SDR and HLG cuts keep their respective color tags; Dolby Vision cuts require explicit `--sdr` because their RPU cannot survive a frame accurate re-encode. Each output path must be new. The automatic joined master is 1920x1080 BT.709 SDR at 30 fps, with HDR tone mapping and orientation-aware fitting. Use `--orientation landscape|portrait|square` to filter mismatched clips, `--include-mismatched` or `--allow <filename>` for intentional exceptions, and `--fit pad|blur|crop` to choose black padding, a blurred copy behind the clip, or crop-to-fill. Read the [edit guide](references/channel-helper.md) before using those commands.

When the final `video.mp4` is present, run `node <bundle>/scripts/cli.mjs transcribe --channel <slug> --draft <id>`. If needed, install the pinned local multilingual `whisper.cpp` with `powershell -File <bundle>/scripts/setup-whisper.ps1`. Pass `--language <code>` when the channel language is known; it avoids auto-detect hallucination on music or ambient audio. Review `transcript.json` and `captions.srt` against the audio and representative frames; correct names, places, and timing. Non-speech annotations are discarded. If the master has no intelligible speech, `transcript.json` records `speech: false` and no `captions.srt` is written; mark the transcript and captions checklist items not applicable with that reason. Draft truthful titles and a description from the transcript, voiceover, or audio context, not just the visuals.

Capture 4-6 representative frames and the spoken or voiceover context. Use an image-generation model to create a **16:9 landscape AI thumbnail** that reflects the actual subject, setting, and hook. Do not use a raw frame with overlaid text as the final thumbnail unless the user explicitly asks. If there is no speech, say so and use ambient audio plus the visible subject; do not invent a spoken hook. Show the generated image for approval before writing `thumbnail.png`. Read [the AI thumbnail workflow](references/thumbnail.md) for the prompt and requirements. Choose background music with `content-studio-music`; it writes `music-selection.json` and `music-credits.txt` after approval. Present the master, title, description, captions, thumbnail, and music credits together for review. Hand the approved package to `content-studio-publish`; publication still needs the user's explicit request.
