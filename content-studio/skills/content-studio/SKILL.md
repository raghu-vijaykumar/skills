# Content Studio

Choose the channel and one draft folder. Read that channel's owner rules and current draft before changing it. For new ideas use `content-studio-ideagen`; choose a treatment with `content-studio-styles`; for Remotion video use `content-studio-videogen`; for raw camera clips use `content-studio-autocut` for a reviewable rough cut or `content-studio-videopreprocess` for clean ordered clips, then use `content-studio-videoedit` for assembly or a supplied final export. Use `content-studio-audio` to master audio in a folder of videos. Choose background music with `content-studio-music` before publishing. Route a reviewed final export to `content-studio-publish`. Use `content-studio-archive` for an independent backup and Drive sync. Each specialist owns its checks; do not skip human review.

An idea is a brief, a reviewed render or export is a publication candidate, and a public YouTube backup is a separate archival state. Keep channel data and media in its content repository, not in this skill bundle. A monthly archive run is user initiated.
