# Instagram Analytics

The existing Instagram route publishes Reels and stores receipts, but the bundle has no Instagram Insights collector. Use an account export, user-supplied Insights capture, or an authorized connected source when available. Publishing credentials alone do not establish analytics access.

Record the account, Reel ID and URL, source and observation time, reporting period, Reel age, and metrics actually present. Treat unavailable metrics as `null`, not zero. Compare Reels with similar age and format; distinguish owned insights from public views, likes and comments. Cite the observed records for any proposed hook, pacing or visual experiment. Follow [the shared record structure](../../references/analytics.md) and keep exports outside this repository. This read-only review does not authorize publication.
