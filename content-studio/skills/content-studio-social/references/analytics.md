# Social analytics record

Store a dated record in the platform's content workspace, outside the skill bundle. Preserve the raw owned export or capture alongside the derived report when rights and storage policy allow. Record the platform, account, collection time, reporting period and timezone, source type and URL or export filename, post ID and URL, format, and which metrics are present. Missing values are `null`, never zero.

Compare posts within the same platform, account, format and comparable age window. Label sample size and avoid attributing performance to a style or topic from a single post. Identify whether a figure is owned analytics, an account export, a public observation, or an editorial inference. Public views, likes and comments do not reveal private impressions, CTR, VTR, retention or audience details.

A recommendation should cite the exact records used, explain the practical uncertainty, and propose one reviewable next experiment. Do not alter a shared visual preset solely from an unreviewed metric report.
