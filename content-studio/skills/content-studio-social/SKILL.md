# Content Studio Social

Choose the platform and operation before opening a specialist skill:

| Platform | Create or prepare | Publish | Analytics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | [Video](../content-studio-video/SKILL.md) and [ideagen](../content-studio-video/planning/ideagen/SKILL.md) | [YouTube Publish](youtube/publish/SKILL.md) | [YouTube Analytics](youtube/analytics/SKILL.md) |
| LinkedIn | [LinkedIn Author](linkedin/author/SKILL.md) | [LinkedIn Publish](linkedin/publish/SKILL.md) | [LinkedIn Analytics](linkedin/analytics/SKILL.md) |
| Instagram | [Video](../content-studio-video/SKILL.md) for a Reel | [Instagram Publish](instagram/publish/SKILL.md) | [Instagram Analytics](instagram/analytics/SKILL.md) |

Production follows the [mandatory precheck](../content-studio-workflow/SKILL.md) and the selected platform's review stages. Publishing has its own explicit authorization. Analytics records the owned data source, observation time and unavailable metrics without changing platform content or conferring publishing permission. The YouTube collector already exists. LinkedIn and Instagram analytics currently work from account exports or other authorized supplied data; no live collector is provided by this skill tree.

For platform-neutral measurement records, use [the analytics structure](references/analytics.md). Keep account data, drafts, receipts and credentials in their personal or channel content workspaces outside this bundle.
