## Workflow

1. **Analyze** — Give me a short list of narration beats, the actor or state change for each, and the evidence. If I selected script analysis only, stop after the beat list.
2. **Human review · Plan** — Before production, run Content Studio precheck. Show the supported style and renderer, exact methods and commands, inputs and outputs, estimates, fallback, and review criteria. Wait for approval of the current revision.
3. **Parallel after plan approval**

   - **Script** — Draft the narration and collect source evidence.
   - **Project** — Check layout and available assets. Verify the geometry and renderer for any Blender insert. Preserve existing approved assets.
4. **Human review · Script and storyboard** — Map the draft narration to visual beats, then show the script, timing, and storyboard. Wait for approval before locking narration and implementing the scenes.
5. **In order** — Align narration to scenes, author editable motion, then render bounded hero and detail previews. Check cue timing, typography, safe margins, transitions, and attribution at delivery size.
6. **Human review · Preview and mix** — Show representative rendered scenes and the narration mix. Revise before rendering the requested full cut.
7. **Human review · Exact master** — Check the delivered picture and audio with me. Return editable source and the requested export. Prepare metadata, thumbnail, or publication only when separately requested.
