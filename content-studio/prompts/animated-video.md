## Workflow in order

1. **Precheck and research** — For analysis-only scope, deliver the requested script analysis and stop. For production, verify claims and outline the hook, teaching beats, and payoff. Show a plan with the compatible preset and renderer, inputs, exact commands, outputs, estimates, fallback, and acceptance criteria. **Human review · Plan** — Approve the plan before production.
2. **Narration first** — Draft and fact-check narration internally, then record or generate the complete audio before animation. Score its hook, clarity, pacing, rehooks, and payoff; correct weak passages and listen through the full track at normal speed. **Human review · Narration audio** — Give me the full audio to listen to and use timecoded feedback for changes. I do not need to read a script. Lock the approved take and cue clock before visual authoring; any later voice change needs renewed listening review and visual retiming.

   - **Narration** — Yes. [Recorded file | generated voice | consented clone], [language, tone, pronunciation]
   - **Music** — No. [Cleared track or mood]
   - **Sound effects (SFX)** — No. [Cue, desired sound, cleared or original source]
   - **Captions** — No. [Language and treatment]

3. **Storyboard and assets on the approved voice clock** — Choose a clear representation for each narrated beat. Collect real-world references for objects and people, including stylized characters. Make original assets or use cleared ones. In the storyboard, list each hero, independently moving part, and action with a stable ID, narration cue, and visible result.

   - **Promotion or CTA** — No. [Offer or action, destination, placement]
   - **New hero assets** — No. [Objects, people, photos, model, or CAD references]
   - **New character rig** — No. [Character and reference photos, expressions, gestures]
   - **3D detail shots** — No. [Cutaways, exploded views, or close-ups and what they show]

   For Blender, use the [code-first 3D workflow](../skills/content-studio-video/animation/engineering-3d/references/code-first-explainer.md). Give each beat a planned time, narration cue, part or pivot, move, camera, and visible end state.

   **Human review · Visual plan** — Review the storyboard and hero references against the approved audio before scene authoring.
4. **Animate, improve, and score** — For Rapid Take, give each spoken word a visible action or state change. Preview each hero and moving part alone, then in the narrated shot at delivery size. Run the [object checkpoint](../skills/content-studio-video/review/critique/references/object-scoring.md#production-checkpoints) for 2D, 2.5D, and 3D. Fix the weakest form, motion, timing, or teaching scores; rerender the affected range and rescore against the same evidence. **Human review · Scene proof** — Review the previews, scores, before/after evidence, and open fixes before full rendering.

   - **Scene transitions** — No. [Scene joins and preferred effect]
   - **Timed graphics** — No. [Labels, pointers, or diagrams and their cues]

5. **Render and review** — Export the requested range or full video with editable sources. Rescore the full hero and action inventory on the encoded video and use the [artifact scorecard](../skills/content-studio-video/review/critique/references/artifact-scoring.md) for the other deliverables. **Human review · Full video** — Watch and listen; give timecoded notes on picture, sound, hooks, rehooks, pacing, teaching clarity, and payoff.
6. **Edit after review** — Apply the requested edits, rerender affected scenes, and rescore the same heroes and actions. Check their joins and narration timing in the complete video. **Human review · Exact master** — Approve the revised render and any unresolved findings. Publishing requires a separate explicit request.
