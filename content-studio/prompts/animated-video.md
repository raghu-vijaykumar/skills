## Workflow in order

1. **Precheck and script analysis** — Deliver `script-analysis.md` with the hook, explanation, and payoff. For each beat, list the time or script line, claim, on-screen action, and evidence or open question. **Human review** — Check the beat sheet. Stop here for analysis-only. For production, approve the plan with compatible preset and renderer, inputs, exact commands, outputs, estimates, fallback, and acceptance criteria.
2. **Script and asset research in parallel** — Draft narration and verify claims. Collect real-world references for objects and people, including stylized characters. Make original assets or use cleared ones. In the storyboard, list each hero, independently moving part, and action with a stable ID, reference, cue, and visible result.

   - **Promotion or CTA** — No. [Offer or action, destination, placement]
   - **New hero assets** — No. [Objects, people, photos, model, or CAD references]
   - **New character rig** — No. [Character and reference photos, expressions, gestures]
   - **3D detail shots** — No. [Cutaways, exploded views, or close-ups and what they show]

   For Blender, use the [code-first 3D workflow](../skills/content-studio-video/animation/engineering-3d/references/code-first-explainer.md). Give each beat a planned time, narration cue, part or pivot, move, camera, and visible end state.

   **Human review · Script and storyboard** — Review the words, visual beats, planned assets, and scores for the hook, rehooks, cadence, and payoff. Stop here if this is the requested scope.
3. **Score narration and lock cues** — Use the [narration review loop](../skills/content-studio-video/review/critique/references/narration-scoring.md) to listen, fix weak passages and rescore. Align the retained take to the on-screen actions. For Rapid Take, give every spoken word a visible action or state change and use vivid focal colors. Time music and effects against the cue sheet.

   - **Narration** — No. [Recorded file | generated voice | consented clone], [delivery notes]
   - **Music** — No. [Cleared track or mood]
   - **Sound effects (SFX)** — No. [Cue, desired sound, cleared or original source]
   - **Captions** — No. [Language and treatment]

4. **Build, score and iterate** — Preview each hero and moving part alone, then in the narrated shot at delivery size. Run the [object checkpoint](../skills/content-studio-video/review/critique/references/object-scoring.md#production-checkpoints) for 2D, 2.5D, and 3D. Fix the lowest axes, rerender the affected range, and rescore against the same evidence. **Human review · Scene proof** — Review the previews, per-part scores, before/after evidence, and open fixes before extending the treatment.

   - **Scene transitions** — No. [Scene joins and preferred effect]
   - **Timed graphics** — No. [Labels, pointers, or diagrams and their cues]

5. **Render and deliver** — Export the requested range or full video with editable sources. Rescore the full hero and action inventory on the encoded master. Use the [artifact scorecard](../skills/content-studio-video/review/critique/references/artifact-scoring.md) for the other deliverables. **Human review · Exact master** — Review per-part score changes and unresolved issues, plus picture, sound, hooks, rehooks, pacing, and payoff. Publishing requires a separate explicit request.

   - **Metadata** — No. [Title and description preferences]
